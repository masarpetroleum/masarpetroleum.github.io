<section class="entry-section">
<p>On October 28, 2024, a ceremony was held at the W Muscat Hotel to mark the renaming of Hydrocarbon Finder to Masar Petroleum. This event brought together employees, partners, and stakeholders to celebrate the company's journey and introduce its new identity, one that embodies its vision and commitment to the future.</p>
<p>The event began with a short film that showcased the history of the company and the story behind its rebranding. Attendees joined an online stream to witness field staff reveal the new logo at an oil field site, a proud moment symbolizing the company&rsquo;s renewed focus on innovation and excellence in energy.</p>
<p style="text-align: center;"><img src="/files/images/8cff4dd2-d513-40b6-a9ed-cb936ea4059d.jpg" alt=""></p>
<p>Following this, the CEO and Chairman addressed the audience with inspiring speeches, highlighting the company&rsquo;s dedication to growth and future ambitions. The speeches concluded with a ceremonial cake-cutting, symbolizing the unity of purpose and forward momentum under the new name.</p>
<p style="text-align: center;"><img src="/files/images/Cake-cut-Masar-Petroleum.jpg" alt=""></p>
<p>Adding an interactive component, guests participated in a puzzle contest and an information quiz about the company's history and milestones. After the activities, attendees enjoyed a celebratory lunch and received special commemorative gifts to remember the occasion.</p>
<p>This event was not only a celebration of a new name but a recognition of shared achievements and a reaffirmation of the company's commitment to progress in the oil and gas industry. As Masar Petroleum, the company looks ahead to a future of impactful work, meaningful partnerships, and continued success.</p>
</section>
