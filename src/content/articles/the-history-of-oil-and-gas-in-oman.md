<h3>Early Developments (Before 1960s)</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Pre-1950s:</strong> Oman had little known petroleum activity. Traditional fishing and agriculture were the main economic activities.</li>
</ul>
<h3>1960s</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>1962:</strong> The first oil exploration concession was awarded to the Petroleum Development Oman (PDO), a consortium of companies led by Shell.</li>
<li><strong>1967:</strong> Commercial oil production began in Oman, with the first oil field, Yibal, coming online.</li>
</ul>
<h3>1970s</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>1970:</strong> Sultan Qaboos bin Said Al Said came to power, and he prioritized modernization and development, including the oil sector.</li>
<li><strong>1976:</strong> The first major oil refinery, the Muscat Refinery, was established, enhancing local refining capabilities.</li>
<li><strong>1979:</strong> Oman&rsquo;s oil production reached 400,000 barrels per day (bpd).</li>
</ul>
<h3>1980s</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>1980:</strong> PDO&rsquo;s production peaked, significantly contributing to the national economy.</li>
<li><strong>1985:</strong> Oil prices fell dramatically due to global market changes, affecting Oman&rsquo;s revenues.</li>
</ul>
<h3>1990s</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>1991:</strong> Oman began to diversify its economy and reduce its dependence on oil revenues.</li>
<li><strong>1996:</strong> The government established the Oman Oil Company to manage oil and gas resources more effectively and to encourage foreign investment.</li>
</ul>
<h3>2000s</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>2000:</strong> Oil production stabilized at around 700,000 bpd, supported by enhanced recovery techniques.</li>
<li><strong>2007:</strong> Oman signed agreements with international oil companies for exploration in the oil-rich areas of the country.</li>
<li><strong>2008:</strong> The natural gas sector began to expand, with significant discoveries being made.</li>
</ul>
<h3>2010s</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>2010:</strong> The government announced the &ldquo;Gas-to-Liquids&rdquo; (GTL) project, which aimed to convert natural gas into liquid fuels.</li>
<li><strong>2012:</strong> The Khazzan gas field was discovered, estimated to hold significant reserves, becoming pivotal for Oman&rsquo;s gas production.</li>
<li><strong>2014:</strong> The government announced plans to further develop the gas sector and expand LNG production capabilities.</li>
</ul>
<h3>2020s</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>2020:</strong> The impact of the COVID-19 pandemic affected global oil prices, prompting Oman to revise its production strategies.</li>
<li><strong>2021:</strong> Oman launched the &ldquo;Oman Vision 2040&rdquo; strategy, emphasizing economic diversification and sustainable development, with a focus on the energy sector.</li>
<li><strong>2023:</strong> Oman made progress in developing renewable energy sources while continuing to invest in oil and gas infrastructure to sustain production levels.</li>
</ul>
<h3>Ongoing Developments</h3>
<ul>
<li>Oman continues to seek foreign investment to develop new oil fields and enhance gas production, including initiatives in sustainability and renewable energy projects.</li>
</ul>
<p>This timeline highlights the major milestones in Oman's oil and gas development, showcasing the evolution of the sector and its significance to the country's economy.</p>
