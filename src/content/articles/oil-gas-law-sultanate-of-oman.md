<section class="entry-section">
<p>Oman's oil and gas sector operates under a clear legal and regulatory framework designed to manage exploration, production, and investment in the energy industry. The Oil &amp; Gas Law outlines the roles, responsibilities, and rights of stakeholders involved in the extraction and utilization of the Sultanate&rsquo;s natural resources. It serves as a foundation for agreements, licensing, and operational guidelines that ensure sustainability, transparency, and alignment with national interests.</p>
<p>For more detailed and official information, you can visit the <a href="https://mem.gov.om/en-us/About-Us/Laws-and-Regulations/Oil-and-Gas" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Ministry of Energy and Minerals website</a>, where related laws and documents are published.</p>
</section>
