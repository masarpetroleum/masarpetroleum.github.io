<h3>Facilities and infrastructure:</h3>
<ul>
<li>Multiple fixed and mobile processing capacity up to 21k gross bpd</li>
<li>Permanent well test facilities available</li>
<li>Fully operational gas compression station</li>
<li>Oil storage capacity of 11k bbls</li>
<li>Produced water injection facilities available</li>
<li>Permanent camp with supporting infrastructure to accommodate 100+ people</li>
<li>4G mobile connectivity</li>
<li>Mechanical and electrical workshops</li>
<li>Permanent ware house and storage yards</li>
</ul>
<p>Additional capex is included in the program to further upgrade the facilities and infrastructure as per growth requirements</p>
<h3>Transportation:</h3>
<ul>
<li>Oil trucked to pumping station for 41 kms and transported through export pipeline for 58 kms</li>
<li>Current export capacity of pipeline up to 6k bopd</li>
</ul>
<h3>Exports:</h3>
<p>Export pipeline connected to PDO main oil line, which transports the oil to export terminal in Mina Al Fahar port in Muscat</p>
