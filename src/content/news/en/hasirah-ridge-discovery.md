A major update from Block 7 has highlighted the scale of the opportunity at Hasirah Ridge, reinforcing the value of Masar Petroleum's disciplined approach to exploration and appraisal.

The finding builds on a sustained programme of seismic interpretation and prospect evaluation, aimed at sharpening drilling confidence before capital is committed. Early indications point to a structure that merits continued appraisal work through the remainder of the year.

Masar Petroleum's technical teams will continue to evaluate the ridge alongside the wider Block 7 portfolio, with further updates to follow as appraisal activity progresses.
