Masar Petroleum has rolled out Starlink-enabled connectivity across its Block 7 field sites, closing a long-standing gap in real-time communication between remote operations and technical teams.

The upgrade allows field crews to coordinate faster with geoscience and engineering staff, and lets specialists provide remote support during time-sensitive operations without waiting on scheduled site visits.

Reliable connectivity is a small but meaningful piece of the company's broader push for disciplined, safety-led field delivery — reducing response times and keeping decision-makers closer to what is happening on the ground.
