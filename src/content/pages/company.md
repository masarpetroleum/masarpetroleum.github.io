<h3><strong>Overview</strong></h3>
<p><em><strong>Masar Petroleum SAOC</strong></em> is a dynamic and forward-thinking oil and gas exploration and production company that began operations in 2015. With a strategic focus on unlocking hydrocarbon resources to meet the world's growing energy demands, Masar Petroleum owns 100% of Block 7 in Sahmah, a significant asset with immense potential for exploration and production activities.</p>
<p>Our company has established a key strategic partnership with S&amp;T, one of the leading contracting firms in the GCC region, further enhancing our operational capabilities. </p>

<hr>

<h3><strong>Mission</strong></h3>
<p>At Masar Petroleum, our mission is to responsibly explore, develop, and produce oil and gas resources, contributing to global energy security while maintaining the highest standards of safety, environmental stewardship, and corporate responsibility. We are dedicated to creating long-term value for our stakeholders and positively impacting the communities where we operate.&nbsp;</p>

<h3>Rebranding from HCF into Masar Petroleum</h3>
<p><img src="/images/files/Masar-field-team.jpg" alt="Masar field team"></p>
<p>In 2024, <em><strong>Hydro Carbon Finder</strong>&nbsp;<strong> E&amp;P</strong> </em>underwent a transformative rebranding, emerging as <em><strong>Masar Petroleum SAOC</strong></em>, a name that embodies our commitment to innovative exploration and production in the oil and gas sector. This strategic change reflects our evolution and ambition to be a leader in responsibly unlocking energy resources to meet the demands of a changing world.</p>
<p>As Masar Petroleum, we continue to strengthen our strategic partnerships to drive operational excellence and create lasting value for our stakeholders and the communities we serve&nbsp;</p>
