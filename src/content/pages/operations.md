<p>The Government of Oman awarded onshore Block 7 (100% ownership) and Block 15 (90% ownership).</p>
<p>Block 7: It is spread across approx. 2,300 sq. km. and contains three producing fields &ndash; Sahmah, Ramlat, and Rija. Production commenced in Sahmah (1980). The block produces light quality oil with average gravity of 44◦ API, which is sold as &lsquo;Oman Blend&rsquo;.</p>
