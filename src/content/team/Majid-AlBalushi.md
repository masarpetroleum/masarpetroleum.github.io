<section class="entry-section">
<p>Majid is a seasoned QHSE Coordinator with over a decade of experience in health, safety, and environmental management. Holding a third secondary education along with NEBOSH and IOSH certifications, he began his career as a mechanic with Saud Bhawna before transitioning into the oil and gas sector, where he served as a roustabout and floorman. His passion for safety led him to join the HSE department, where he now plays a critical role in promoting a culture of safety and compliance.</p>
<p>In his current role, Majid is responsible for overseeing safe work systems, updating HSE policies and procedures, leading incident investigations, and submitting monthly HSE performance reports to the ministry. As a member of OSHEMCO under OPAL, he actively contributes to the development of national safety standards and regularly conducts awareness sessions to promote best practices across the workforce.</p>
<p>Majid's notable accomplishments include maintaining a seven-year streak without any lost-time injuries and playing a key role in supporting his company&rsquo;s successful ISO certification process. He is a certified Lead Auditor in ISO 45001 (Occupational Health &amp; Safety), ISO 9001 (Quality Management), and ISO 14001 (Environmental Management). In addition, he has received specialized training in coaching, radiation protection, and on-scene command operations.</p>
<p>His responsibilities also include conducting focused inspections in high-risk areas and managing critical safety-related permissions and licenses to ensure safe and uninterrupted operations.</p>
